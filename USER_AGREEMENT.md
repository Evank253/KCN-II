and the KCN-II operator ("KCN") for use of Ketchum's Intelligent Investigator.

**This is a software terms document, not legal advice.**

## 1. Acceptance
You accept this Agreement, the License, and the Legal Agreement when you tap
**AUTHORIZE ACCESS**, check the acceptance box, or otherwise use KCN-II.

If you do not agree, do not use the console.

## 2. Who may use it
You must be 18 or older. You use KCN-II as an independent researcher or
investigator. You are not granted police, court, or intelligence-agency power.

## 3. Your responsibilities
You agree to:

- Use the console only for lawful, good-faith investigation and research.
- Review every automatic extraction, lookup, and search suggestion yourself.
- Not upload material you have no right to handle.
- Not use scans, lookups, or swarm searches to stalk, harass, or harm anyone.
- Keep credentials, case files, and source material under your control.
- Comply with applicable law in every jurisdiction you work in.

## 4. Case data
KCN-II stores case text, scans, notes, and related records in your browser
unless you export them. You are the data controller of your case file.

Do not treat local storage as an evidence vault, chain-of-custody system, or
backup. Export and archive important files yourself.

## 5. Camera, microphone, and files
If you grant camera, microphone, or file access, that media is processed on
your device and, when you run a lookup, may be sent to an AI service to follow
your instruction. You are responsible for what you capture and submit.

## 6. Lookups and AI output
Lookups, OCR, entity extraction, and conversation replies are **leads for human
review**. They can be wrong, incomplete, or outdated. They are not findings,
not testimony, and not legal conclusions.

## 7. Public-source searches
Web swarm links open public search engines. KCN does not control those sites.
You are responsible for how you use third-party results.
